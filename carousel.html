Takeaway 1 of 5

Personal isn’t human. Difference is the value.

AI makes every service individually better and collectively the same. When sameness is cheap, difference lives in interaction, in the tensions a company chooses to hold, and in the room it leaves for a person.

Takeaway 2 of 5

Feel it. Design belongs closer to the decision.

Feels, reason and data all lead to product decisions. A design team that only brings data is not in the room where the call is made, and in agentic products the question becomes who is allowed to say yes.

Takeaway 3 of 5

Six months with an agent. Detach, focus, align, delegate, audit.

Sures Kumar gave an agent his calendar, fifteen years of email, his finances and his lab results. Every failure fell into one of three buckets: missing context, information that can’t be captured, or bad reasoning with everything in hand.

Sures Kumar, Google DeepMind · Gleb Kuznetsov, Brain Technologies · Abhilash Kolapareddy, Citi · Coral Miniel, fixurfeed

Takeaway 4 of 5

The best design system is one AI can’t forget to use.

Design systems are turning from documentation into living infrastructure that humans and AI both read, so generative tools inherit a company’s design language by default. The designers who win orchestrate context and taste.

Ivan Barriga, FlavorCloud · Jonathan Ruiz, Docusign · Dianne Alter, The Design Project · Raul Justiniano, Asurion

Takeaway 5 of 5

Research at the scale of the business, judgment in the loop.

One researcher, 200,000 open-ended responses a quarter and an AI-assisted synthesis pipeline, with a human check for the costly failure: feedback that sounds identical but points to different root causes.
